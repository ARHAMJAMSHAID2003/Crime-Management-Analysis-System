The Crime Management Analysis System (CMAS), also known as MehfoozPakistan, is a comprehensive data driven platform designed to modernize crime reporting, investigation tracking, and predictive policing in Pakistan. The primary objective is to provide law enforcement agencies with a centralized system that streamlines crime management while enabling data driven decision making through advanced analytics and pattern detection. Built on a React.js frontend and Node.js/Express backend with Oracle Database integration, CMAS features role based access control for officers, victims, and witnesses, ensuring secure collaboration across all stakeholders. The system manages the complete crime lifecycle from initial reporting and evidence tracking to investigation management and suspect profiling. Its analytical capabilities include crime hotspot identification, trend analysis, and predictive risk assessment using historical data, empowering law enforcement to proactively address crime patterns and enhance operational efficiency for public safety.

Crime Analysis
This workflow begins when an officer logs into the Crime Management and Analysis System and navigates to the analytics dashboard which displays real-time crime statistics and analytical tools for pattern identification and risk assessment. The officer accesses the crime hotspots feature which queries the database to retrieve and display the top high-crime locations ranked by total crime count, showing each location's city, area, street details, total number of crimes, number of solved cases, and solve rate percentage, allowing the officer to identify which geographical areas require the most attention and resources. The officer can specify a limit parameter to view the top 10, 20, or 50 hotspot locations based on their analytical needs. Next, the officer examines crime trends by applying filters for specific years, months, or crime types, and the system returns trend data showing how crime rates have changed over time using advanced database window functions that rank and compare crime statistics across different time periods, helping identify whether crime is increasing, decreasing, or remaining stable in particular areas or categories. To understand temporal patterns, the officer accesses the crime patterns module which analyzes all crimes by day of week and time of day, revealing patterns such as which days experience the highest crime rates and what hours of the day are most dangerous, providing insights like weekends having more violent crimes or late night hours being peak times for certain offenses. The officer then reviews category distribution statistics which break down the total crime portfolio showing how many crimes fall into each category including Violent, Property, Cyber, White_Collar, and Drug_Related crimes, giving a comprehensive overview of the crime landscape and helping prioritize which types of crimes need more investigative resources. For performance evaluation, the officer accesses the officer performance analytics which display statistics for all officers including their case counts, resolution rates, and effectiveness rankings, helping identify high-performing officers and those who may need additional support or training. When planning resource deployment, the officer uses the crime risk prediction feature by inputting a specific city and optionally an area, and the system calculates a risk score for that location based on historical crime data, recent crime activity within the last 30 days, and solve rate performance, then categorizes the location as HIGH, MEDIUM, or LOW risk, enabling data-driven decisions about where to increase patrol presence or investigative focus. The officer can also search for similar crime patterns by providing parameters such as crime type, location details, or day of week, and the system identifies other crimes with matching characteristics, which is valuable for connecting related incidents, identifying serial offenders, or recognizing emerging criminal trends that span multiple cases. For strategic planning, the officer utilizes the crime forecasting feature which projects future crime trends for specified months ahead based on historical patterns, allowing the department to anticipate seasonal increases, prepare for potential crime waves, and proactively allocate resources before problems escalate. The officer accesses time series data which provides chart-ready information grouped by day, week, or month within specified date ranges, showing crime frequency over time and making it easy to visualize trends, spikes, and patterns in graphical format for presentations or reports. Throughout the analysis process, the officer can apply various filters including date ranges, crime types, geographical areas, and severity levels to drill down into specific aspects of the crime data and generate focused insights for particular neighborhoods, crime categories, or time periods. Based on all these analytical insights including hotspot identification showing where crimes are concentrated, trend analysis revealing whether crime is rising or falling, pattern recognition identifying peak times and days for criminal activity, risk predictions highlighting which areas need immediate attention, and performance metrics showing investigation effectiveness, the officer develops strategic recommendations for the department such as suggesting increased patrols in high-risk areas during identified peak hours, recommending targeted investigations in locations with low solve rates, identifying areas where crime prevention efforts should be focused based on rising trends, and proposing resource reallocation to maximize crime reduction impact. The officer shares these findings with department leadership and fellow officers through briefings and discussions, using the analytical data to justify deployment decisions, support budget requests for additional resources in high-crime areas, and demonstrate the effectiveness of data-driven policing strategies in improving public safety and optimizing law enforcement operations.
