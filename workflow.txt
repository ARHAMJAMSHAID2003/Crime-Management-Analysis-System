The Crime Management Analysis System (CMAS), also known as MehfoozPakistan, is a comprehensive data driven platform designed to modernize crime reporting, investigation tracking, and predictive policing in Pakistan. The primary objective is to provide law enforcement agencies with a centralized system that streamlines crime management while enabling data driven decision making through advanced analytics and pattern detection. Built on a React.js frontend and Node.js/Express backend with Oracle Database integration, CMAS features role based access control for officers, victims, and witnesses, ensuring secure collaboration across all stakeholders. The system manages the complete crime lifecycle from initial reporting and evidence tracking to investigation management and suspect profiling. Its analytical capabilities include crime hotspot identification, trend analysis, and predictive risk assessment using historical data, empowering law enforcement to proactively address crime patterns and enhance operational efficiency for public safety.

Victim Crime Reporting and Officer Review & Investigation Initiation:
This workflow begins when a victim accesses the Crime Pattern Analysis System through the public portal and clicks on the "Report a Crime" option where they are prompted to either login with existing credentials or create a new account by providing their personal information including name, age, gender, contact details, address, email, and password which is securely hashed using bcrypt. After successful registration and login, the victim is directed to the crime reporting form where they fill in comprehensive details including selecting the crime type from a dropdown menu of predefined categories (Violent, Property, Cyber, White_Collar, Drug_Related, Other), specifying the date and time when the incident occurred, providing the exact location details with city, area, and street address, writing a detailed narrative description of the incident including what happened and any identifying information about perpetrators, optionally uploading supporting evidence such as photos, videos, or documents, and submitting the report which generates a unique Report_ID and stores it in the Crime_Report table with status "Pending Review". The system sends a confirmation notification to the victim with their report ID for tracking purposes. Meanwhile, on the officer portal side, authenticated officers log into their dashboard using their official credentials where they can see a dedicated section showing all pending crime reports that require review sorted by date and severity. An officer selects a specific crime report to examine and the system displays the complete details including victim information, incident description, date and time, location, evidence attachments, and initial assessment parameters. The officer carefully reviews the report to determine its validity and authenticity by cross-referencing with existing crime patterns, checking for duplicate reports, validating location details, and assessing the severity and credibility of the claim. Based on this evaluation, the officer has two options: if the report appears suspicious, lacks sufficient detail, or seems fraudulent, the officer can reject it by selecting "Reject Report" and providing a reason which updates the Crime_Report status to "Rejected" and notifies the victim with the rejection reason; alternatively, if the officer determines the report is legitimate and requires action, they click "Accept and Create Investigation" which triggers a multi-step automated process. Upon acceptance, the system first creates an official Crime record in the Crime table by extracting information from the crime report including crime_type_id, date_occurred, date_reported (current timestamp), location_id, description, status (set to "Open"), severity_level (assigned by officer or auto-determined), and officer_id (the accepting officer), then automatically generates a unique Crime_ID. Subsequently, the system creates a formal Investigation record by generating a unique case number following the format "CASE-YYYY-####", setting the investigation status to "Active", assigning the current officer as the lead investigator, recording the start date as the current date, setting the outcome as "Pending", and creating an Investigation_ID. The system then establishes relationships by inserting records into the Report_Crime bridge table to link the original report to the newly created crime, and into the Investigation_Crime bridge table to associate the crime with the investigation, and into the Crime_Victim bridge table to officially link the victim to the crime record. The officer is then prompted to add additional investigation details including priority level (High, Medium, Low), expected completion timeline, initial investigation notes, and option to assign additional officers to the investigation team. Once the investigation is officially created, the system performs several automated actions: updates the original Crime_Report status from "Pending Review" to "Under Investigation", sends email notifications to the victim informing them that their report has been accepted and an official investigation has been initiated with the case number and assigned officer details, logs all actions in the audit trail for accountability, updates the officer's dashboard statistics showing assigned cases and workload, and allows the victim to track their case status in real-time by logging into their account and viewing investigation progress, evidence collection status, suspect identification updates, and receiving notifications when the officer needs additional information or when significant milestones are achieved in the investigation.
